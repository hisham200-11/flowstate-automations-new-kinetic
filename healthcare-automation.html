02 // HEALTHCARE FAQ

Answers for Medical Directors & Clinic Owners.

Practical answers on deploying automated appointment systems in Philippine clinics.

Can it handle multiple branches and multiple doctors with different schedules?

Yes. The system organizes consultations by branch location, medical specialty, and doctor schedule. Patients can choose their preferred branch and doctor seamlessly.

How does it answer HMO and health card coverage questions?

FlowState is trained on your exact accredited HMO list (e.g. Maxicare, Intellicare, Medicard, PhilCare) and clarifies which specific doctors accept specific HMO cards.

Do you charge per doctor or per patient booking?

No. We build on your dedicated private cloud architecture with zero monthly per-doctor or per-booking license fees.
